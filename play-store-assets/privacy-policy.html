1. Overview & 100% Ad-Free Commitment

Hydroponics Step by Step ("the App") is a free, open-science hydroponic nutrient calculator, 3-course step-by-step training academy (50 lessons), and BARI cultivation handbook built for farmers, students, researchers, and growers in Bangladesh and worldwide. The App is 100% Ad-Free: it contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, displays zero banner or interstitial advertisements, and explicitly removes the Android Advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID).

2. Information Collection and Use (Zero Data Collected)

We do not collect, store on external servers, transmit, rent, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII) or user device data:

  • No Account Required: You never need to register, sign in, or provide your name, email address, or phone number.
  • 100% Local On-Device Storage: All nutrient formulas, custom fertilizer salts, water quality profiles, course completion progress, custom language translations, and farm batch logs are saved exclusively on your own device using local application storage (AsyncStorage).
  • No Analytics or Tracking SDKs: The App does not include third-party tracking scripts, crash telemetry, or behavioral profiling tools.
  • No Sensitive Permissions: The App does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photo gallery, or external file storage.

3. Children's Privacy & Target Audience

Hydroponics Step by Step is an agricultural and scientific calculation and training utility intended for farmers, agronomists, university students, and adult growers. Because the App collects zero personal data from any user, no personal information from children or minors is ever collected or processed.

4. Scientific Attribution & Safe Agricultural Handling

The mathematical nutrient solver, Limiting Molar Conductivity (LMCv2) EC estimation engine, and core fertilizer datasets are based on Dr. Daniel Fernandez's open-source HydroBuddy v1.100 (https://github.com/danielfppps/hydrobuddy) licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0), combined with 3 complete step-by-step courses and localized Bangladesh Agricultural Research Institute (BARI) hydroponic crop guides. When handling concentrated agricultural acids in real life, always wear protective equipment and add acid to water.
